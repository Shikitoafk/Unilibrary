UniLibrary
Detailed breakdowns of universities so students can figure out whether a university actually fits them — not by ranking, but by matching what the school offers to what the student needs.

Why
I spent months researching universities for my own applications. Most information was scattered, translated badly, or written as ads. UniLibrary is the resource I wished existed: structured, honest, in Russian.

Status
Live. https://unilibrary-green.vercel.app/
